## Claim boundaries that must not drift

1. A drift crossing is not an outcome crossing; an outcome crossing is not a causal-starvation certificate without weak-only first-hit learnability.
2. The terminal beta profile is post-hoc, read-only, and diagnostic. It does not replace the preregistered first-hit gate and does not rank architectures.
3. E19 failed its complete preregistered five-endpoint rule. E20 passed only drift/response event classification at one frozen synthetic cell.
4. E20 does not validate phase, causal starvation, learnability, trajectory magnitude, calibrated time, prevalence, or kernel stability.
5. Artifact integrity does not imply historical replayability or scientific validity.
6. Proposition 5 remains unpromoted unless separately proved and entered into the authority chain.
7. No real-data, semi-real, Waterbirds, expanded nonlinear, prospective-beta, or CDC-tradeoff result may be added without a genuinely executed, scoped, auditable experiment. The two frozen designs currently have zero outcomes and no E-claim IDs.
8. T19's exact threshold is initialization-conditioned and realized-geometry-specific. Its trajectory implications require independently proved uniform product-ball constants; sampled trajectories do not supply them.
9. T20 applies only at zero bulk gain, zero lag, and zero background noise. It establishes immediate suppression, not transfer-then-suppression, and is not a positive-disorder phase boundary.
10. The Bloop-style shadow-target rescue and PCGrad-style shadow-target rescue labels must not be shortened into canonical algorithm claims without parity audits.
